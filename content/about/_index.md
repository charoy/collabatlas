CollabAtlas is an open, community-governed knowledge platform that catalogues, connects, and contextualizes collaborative solutions across domains, methods, tools, and research approaches.

## Why CollabAtlas?

Researchers and practitioners working on collaboration face a fragmented landscape. Methods are scattered across academic papers, tools across blog posts, and case studies across conference proceedings. There is no unified resource that connects these dimensions and enables cross-domain discovery.

CollabAtlas addresses this by providing a structured, multi-dimensional catalogue where every entry is connected to others through shared taxonomies — enabling you to discover relevant approaches you might never have found otherwise.

## Principles

- **Open and collaborative** — the platform is itself a collaborative artifact
- **Reliable and curated** — editorial board governance ensures quality
- **Built to last** — zero infrastructure costs, Git-based, fully forkable
- **Practical** — decision frameworks, templates, and comparison tools alongside the catalogue
- **Multi-dimensional** — entries connected across domains, scales, methods, and modalities
