Social-Ecological Systems Framework is a framework for collaborative work. Analytical framework for understanding interactions between social and ecological systems in governance contexts. It is particularly relevant in Environmental Science, Agriculture and Public Policy.

Social-Ecological Systems Framework supports interdisciplinary, transdisciplinary and community-based collaboration and is suited for multi-organization networks and community-scale initiatives in hybrid settings.

Social-Ecological Systems Framework is a well-documented framework with extensive evidence, guides, and community support.
