---
title: "Quadruple Helix Innovation"
data_id: "quadruple-helix-innovation"
---

Quadruple Helix Innovation is a framework for collaborative work. Extension of Triple Helix adding civil society as fourth actor in innovation ecosystems. It is particularly relevant in Public Policy, Business, Education and Urban Planning.

Quadruple Helix Innovation supports transdisciplinary, co-creation and community-based collaboration and is suited for multi-organization networks and community-scale initiatives in hybrid settings.

As an emerging framework, Quadruple Helix Innovation is still developing its evidence base and community adoption.
