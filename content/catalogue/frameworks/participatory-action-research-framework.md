Participatory Action Research Framework is a framework for collaborative work. Systematic framework for community members to investigate and take action on issues affecting their lives. It is particularly relevant in Social Sciences, Education, Healthcare and Agriculture.

Participatory Action Research Framework supports participatory, community-based and co-production collaboration and is suited for small teams, organizational settings and community-scale initiatives in in-person and hybrid settings.

Participatory Action Research Framework is classified as a well-documented framework, indicating broad adoption and available documentation.
