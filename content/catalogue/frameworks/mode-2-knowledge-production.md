Mode 2 Knowledge Production is a framework for collaborative work. Framework describing transdisciplinary, application-driven knowledge creation outside traditional academic boundaries. It is particularly relevant in Social Sciences, Education and Public Policy.

Mode 2 Knowledge Production supports transdisciplinary, interdisciplinary and co-production collaboration and is suited for organizational settings and multi-organization networks in hybrid settings.

Mode 2 Knowledge Production is classified as a well-documented framework, indicating broad adoption and available documentation.
