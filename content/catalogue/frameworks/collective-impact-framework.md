Collective Impact Framework is a framework for collaborative work. Structured approach for cross-sector collaboration to address complex social problems through shared agenda. It is particularly relevant in Public Policy, Healthcare, Education and Urban Planning.

Collective Impact Framework supports co-production, community-based and transdisciplinary collaboration and is suited for multi-organization networks and community-scale initiatives in in-person and hybrid settings.

Collective Impact Framework is classified as a well-documented framework, indicating broad adoption and available documentation.
