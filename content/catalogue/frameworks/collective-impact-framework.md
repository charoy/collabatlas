Collective Impact Framework is a framework for collaborative work. Structured approach for cross-sector collaboration to address complex social problems through shared agenda. It is particularly relevant in Public Policy, Healthcare and Education.

Collective Impact Framework supports co-production, community-based and transdisciplinary collaboration and is suited for multi-organization networks and community-scale initiatives in in-person and hybrid settings.

Collective Impact Framework is a well-documented framework with extensive evidence, guides, and community support.
