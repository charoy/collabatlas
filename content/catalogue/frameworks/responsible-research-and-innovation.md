Responsible Research and Innovation is a framework for collaborative work. EU framework for inclusive and sustainable research through anticipation, reflexivity, inclusion, and responsiveness. It is particularly relevant in Public Policy, Environmental Science, Healthcare and Social Sciences.

Responsible Research and Innovation supports participatory, transdisciplinary and co-design collaboration and is suited for multi-organization networks and community-scale initiatives in hybrid settings.

Responsible Research and Innovation is an established framework with a solid track record of use across multiple contexts.
