Agile Methodology is a framework for collaborative work. Iterative development framework emphasizing adaptive planning, cross-functional teams, and continuous delivery. It is particularly relevant in Software Engineering, Business and Design.

Agile Methodology supports co-production, distributed and co-design collaboration and is suited for small teams and organizational settings in in-person, remote and hybrid settings.

Agile Methodology is classified as a well-documented framework, indicating broad adoption and available documentation.
