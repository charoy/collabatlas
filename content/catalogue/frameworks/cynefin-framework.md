Cynefin Framework is a framework for collaborative work. Sense-making framework that helps teams choose appropriate collaboration strategies based on problem complexity. It is particularly relevant in Business, Public Policy and Disaster Response.

Cynefin Framework supports co-design and distributed collaboration and is suited for small teams and organizational settings in in-person and hybrid settings.

Cynefin Framework is a well-documented framework with extensive evidence, guides, and community support.
