Global Biodiversity Information Facility is a dataset for collaborative work. International open data infrastructure providing access to over 2 billion species occurrence records. It is particularly relevant in Environmental Science, Citizen Science and Agriculture.

Global Biodiversity Information Facility supports open source, distributed and community-based collaboration and is suited for multi-organization networks and community-scale initiatives in remote settings.

Global Biodiversity Information Facility is a well-documented dataset with extensive evidence, guides, and community support.
