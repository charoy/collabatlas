Humanitarian Data Exchange is a dataset for collaborative work. Open platform for sharing humanitarian data across organizations responding to crises worldwide. It is particularly relevant in Disaster Response, Public Policy and Healthcare.

Humanitarian Data Exchange supports distributed, open source and community-based collaboration and is suited for multi-organization networks and community-scale initiatives in remote settings.

Humanitarian Data Exchange is an established dataset with a solid track record of use across multiple contexts.
