The Stack Overflow Developer Survey is one of the most comprehensive annual surveys of software developers worldwide, collecting data on demographics, education, employment, technology preferences, and work practices. Conducted since 2011, the survey consistently attracts tens of thousands of respondents from across the global developer community. The resulting datasets provide rich longitudinal insights into trends in programming languages, frameworks, tools, and developer satisfaction. Researchers and industry analysts widely use this data to study workforce dynamics, technology adoption patterns, and the evolving nature of software development work. It is particularly relevant in Software Engineering and Education.

Stack Overflow Developer Survey supports crowdsourcing and community-based collaboration and is suited for community-scale initiatives in remote settings.

Stack Overflow Developer Survey is a well-documented dataset with extensive evidence, guides, and community support. The dataset is hosted on kaggle, in CSV format.
