OpenAlex Research Graph is a dataset for collaborative work. Open catalog of scholarly works, authors, institutions, and concepts replacing Microsoft Academic Graph. It is particularly relevant in Social Sciences, Education and Publishing.

OpenAlex Research Graph supports open source, distributed and interdisciplinary collaboration and is suited for community-scale initiatives in remote settings.

OpenAlex Research Graph is an established dataset with a solid track record of use across multiple contexts.
