European Social Survey is a dataset for collaborative work. Cross-national survey measuring attitudes, beliefs, and behaviour across 30+ European countries biennially. It is particularly relevant in Social Sciences, Public Policy and Education.

European Social Survey supports interdisciplinary, distributed and co-production collaboration and is suited for multi-organization networks in hybrid settings.

European Social Survey is a well-documented dataset with extensive evidence, guides, and community support.
