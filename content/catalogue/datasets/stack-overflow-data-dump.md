Stack Overflow Data Dump is a dataset for collaborative work. Anonymized dump of all questions, answers, and user interactions from the developer Q&A platform. It is particularly relevant in Software Engineering and Education.

Stack Overflow Data Dump supports crowdsourcing, community-based and distributed collaboration and is suited for community-scale initiatives in remote settings.

Stack Overflow Data Dump is classified as a well-documented dataset, indicating broad adoption and available documentation.
