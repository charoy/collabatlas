Appreciative Inquiry is a method for collaborative work. Strengths-based change methodology that engages stakeholders in discovering and building on organizational assets. It is particularly relevant in Business, Education and Healthcare.

Appreciative Inquiry supports participatory and co-creation collaboration and is suited for small teams and organizational settings in in-person and hybrid settings.

Appreciative Inquiry is classified as a well-documented method, indicating broad adoption and available documentation.
