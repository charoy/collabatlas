Hackathon is a method for collaborative work. Time-bounded collaborative event where diverse teams rapidly prototype solutions to defined challenges. It is particularly relevant in Software Engineering, Design, Citizen Science and Healthcare.

Hackathon supports co-creation, interdisciplinary and crowdsourcing collaboration and is suited for small teams, organizational settings and community-scale initiatives in in-person, remote and hybrid settings.

Hackathon is classified as a well-documented method, indicating broad adoption and available documentation.
