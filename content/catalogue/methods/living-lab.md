title: "Living Lab"
data_id: "living-lab"
---

Living Lab is a method for collaborative work. Open innovation ecosystem operating in real-world settings where users co-create solutions with researchers. It is particularly relevant in Urban Planning, Design, Software Engineering and Healthcare.

Living Lab supports co-creation, co-design and transdisciplinary collaboration and is suited for organizational settings, multi-organization networks and community-scale initiatives in in-person and hybrid settings.

Living Lab is an established method with a solid track record of use across multiple contexts.
