Nominal Group Technique is a method for collaborative work. Structured decision-making method combining individual idea generation with group ranking and discussion. It is particularly relevant in Healthcare, Business and Education.

Nominal Group Technique supports participatory and interdisciplinary collaboration and is suited for small teams in in-person and remote settings.

Nominal Group Technique is classified as a well-documented method, indicating broad adoption and available documentation.
