---
title: "Citizen Assembly"
data_id: "citizen-assembly"
---

Citizen Assembly is a method for collaborative work. Deliberative democratic method where randomly selected citizens discuss and make policy recommendations. It is particularly relevant in Public Policy, Urban Planning and Environmental Science.

Citizen Assembly supports participatory, community-based and transdisciplinary collaboration and is suited for organizational settings and community-scale initiatives in in-person and hybrid settings.

Citizen Assembly is an established method with a solid track record of use across multiple contexts.
