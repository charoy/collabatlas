Galaxy Zoo is a case study for collaborative work. Citizen science project where volunteers classify galaxies from telescope images to advance astronomy research. It is particularly relevant in Citizen Science and Education.

Galaxy Zoo supports crowdsourcing, distributed and community-based collaboration and is suited for community-scale initiatives in remote settings.

Galaxy Zoo is classified as a well-documented case study, indicating broad adoption and available documentation. This case study examines real-world application of collaborative approaches.
