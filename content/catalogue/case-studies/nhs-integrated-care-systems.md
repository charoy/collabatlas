NHS Integrated Care Systems is a case study for collaborative work. UK health system partnerships bringing together providers and commissioners to plan services collaboratively. It is particularly relevant in Healthcare and Public Policy.

NHS Integrated Care Systems supports co-production, interdisciplinary and transdisciplinary collaboration and is suited for multi-organization networks and community-scale initiatives in hybrid settings.

NHS Integrated Care Systems is an established case study with a solid track record of use across multiple contexts. This case study examines real-world application of collaborative approaches.
