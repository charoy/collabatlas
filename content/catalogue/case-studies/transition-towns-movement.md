Transition Towns Movement supports community-based, participatory and co-creation collaboration and is suited for community-scale initiatives in in-person and hybrid settings.

Transition Towns Movement is an established case study with a solid track record of use across multiple contexts. This case study demonstrates real-world application of collaborative approaches.
