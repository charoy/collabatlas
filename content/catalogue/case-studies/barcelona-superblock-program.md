Barcelona Superblock Program supports participatory, co-design and community-based collaboration and is suited for organizational settings and community-scale initiatives in in-person and hybrid settings.

Barcelona Superblock Program is an established case study with a solid track record of use across multiple contexts. This case study examines real-world application of collaborative approaches.
