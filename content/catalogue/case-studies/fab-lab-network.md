Fab Lab Network is a case study for collaborative work. Global network of community fabrication laboratories enabling local digital manufacturing and collaborative innovation. It is particularly relevant in Design, Manufacturing and Education.

Fab Lab Network supports open source, community-based and co-creation collaboration and is suited for small teams and community-scale initiatives in in-person settings.

Fab Lab Network is an established case study with a solid track record of use across multiple contexts. This case study examines real-world application of collaborative approaches.
