Medellín Urban Innovation supports participatory, co-design and transdisciplinary collaboration and is suited for multi-organization networks and community-scale initiatives in in-person settings.

Medellín Urban Innovation is a well-documented case study with extensive evidence, guides, and community support. This case study demonstrates real-world application of collaborative approaches.
