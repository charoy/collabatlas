Auroville Experimental Township is a case study for collaborative work. International community in India experimenting with collective governance and sustainable living. It is particularly relevant in Urban Planning, Education, Arts & Culture and Agriculture.

Auroville Experimental Township supports community-based, co-creation and transdisciplinary collaboration and is suited for community-scale initiatives in in-person settings.

Auroville Experimental Township is an established case study with a solid track record of use across multiple contexts. This case study examines real-world application of collaborative approaches.
