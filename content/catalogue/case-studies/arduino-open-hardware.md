Arduino Open Hardware is a case study for collaborative work. Open-source electronics platform built by a global community of makers, educators, and engineers. It is particularly relevant in Design, Education, Manufacturing and Software Engineering.

Arduino Open Hardware supports open source, co-creation and community-based collaboration and is suited for community-scale initiatives in remote and hybrid settings.

Arduino Open Hardware is classified as a well-documented case study, indicating broad adoption and available documentation. This case study examines real-world application of collaborative approaches.
