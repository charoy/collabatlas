Arduino Open Hardware is a case study for collaborative work. Open-source electronics platform built by a global community of makers, educators, and engineers. It is particularly relevant in Design, Education and Manufacturing.

Arduino Open Hardware supports open source, co-creation and community-based collaboration and is suited for community-scale initiatives in remote and hybrid settings.

Arduino Open Hardware is a well-documented case study with extensive evidence, guides, and community support. This case study demonstrates real-world application of collaborative approaches.
