Mondragon Cooperative is a case study for collaborative work. Worker-owned cooperative federation in Spain demonstrating collaborative governance in business at scale. It is particularly relevant in Business, Manufacturing and Education.

Mondragon Cooperative supports co-production, community-based and participatory collaboration and is suited for organizational settings and multi-organization networks in in-person and hybrid settings.

Mondragon Cooperative is a well-documented case study with extensive evidence, guides, and community support. This case study demonstrates real-world application of collaborative approaches.
