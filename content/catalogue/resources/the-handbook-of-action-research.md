The Handbook of Action Research is a resource for collaborative work. Comprehensive reference covering participatory inquiry theory, practice, and applications across disciplines. It is particularly relevant in Social Sciences, Education and Healthcare.

The Handbook of Action Research supports participatory, co-production and community-based collaboration and is suited for small teams and organizational settings in in-person and hybrid settings.

The Handbook of Action Research is classified as a well-documented resource, indicating broad adoption and available documentation. This resource provides valuable reference material for researchers and practitioners.
