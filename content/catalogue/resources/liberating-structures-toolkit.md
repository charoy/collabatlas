Liberating Structures Toolkit is a resource for collaborative work. Collection of 33 facilitation microstructures for engaging everyone in shaping group decisions. It is particularly relevant in Business, Education and Healthcare.

Liberating Structures Toolkit supports participatory and co-creation collaboration and is suited for small teams and organizational settings in in-person and hybrid settings.

Liberating Structures Toolkit is a well-documented resource with extensive evidence, guides, and community support. This resource provides valuable reference material for researchers and practitioners.
