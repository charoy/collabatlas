InnerSource Patterns is a resource for collaborative work. Documented patterns for applying open-source collaboration practices within organizational boundaries. It is particularly relevant in Software Engineering and Business.

InnerSource Patterns supports open source, distributed and co-production collaboration and is suited for organizational settings and multi-organization networks in remote settings.

As an emerging resource, InnerSource Patterns is still developing its evidence base and community adoption. This resource provides valuable reference material for researchers and practitioners.
