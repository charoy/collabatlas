Jitsi Meet is a tool for collaborative work. Open-source video conferencing platform for secure, self-hosted online meetings and collaboration. It is particularly relevant in Software Engineering, Education and Healthcare.

Jitsi Meet supports open source and distributed collaboration and is suited for pair work, small teams and organizational settings in remote settings.

Jitsi Meet is an established tool with a solid track record of use across multiple contexts.
