Figma is a cloud-based collaborative design tool that allows teams to create user interfaces, prototypes, and design systems in real time. Multiple designers and stakeholders can work simultaneously on the same file, with changes visible instantly to all participants. Figma supports the full design workflow from wireframing to high-fidelity prototyping, and includes features like shared component libraries, design tokens, and developer handoff tools. It integrates with popular platforms such as Slack, Jira, and Miro to streamline the product design process. It is particularly relevant in Design, Software Engineering and Education.

Figma supports co-design, co-creation and distributed collaboration and is suited for pair work, small teams and organizational settings in remote and hybrid settings.

Figma is a well-documented tool with extensive evidence, guides, and community support. It is a proprietary platform, available on Web, macOS, Windows.
