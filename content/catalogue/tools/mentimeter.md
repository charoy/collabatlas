Mentimeter is a tool for collaborative work. Interactive presentation tool with live polls, quizzes, and word clouds for audience engagement. It is particularly relevant in Education, Business and Public Policy.

Mentimeter supports participatory and crowdsourcing collaboration and is suited for small teams, organizational settings and community-scale initiatives in in-person, remote and hybrid settings.

Mentimeter is an established tool with a solid track record of use across multiple contexts.
