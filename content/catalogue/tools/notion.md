Notion is a versatile all-in-one workspace that combines note-taking, document creation, knowledge management, and project management into a single platform. Teams can collaboratively build wikis, manage tasks with kanban boards and databases, and create shared documentation with rich formatting and embedded content. Notion supports real-time editing and commenting, making it suitable for both synchronous and asynchronous collaboration. Its flexible block-based architecture allows users to customize pages and workflows to fit a wide variety of organizational needs. It is particularly relevant in Business, Education and Software Engineering.

Notion supports distributed and co-creation collaboration and is suited for small teams and organizational settings in remote and hybrid settings.

Notion is classified as a well-documented tool, indicating broad adoption and available documentation. It is a proprietary platform, available on Web, macOS, Windows, iOS.
