Nextcloud is a tool for collaborative work. Self-hosted collaboration platform providing file sharing, calendars, and real-time document editing. It is particularly relevant in Software Engineering, Education and Healthcare.

Nextcloud supports open source, distributed and co-production collaboration and is suited for small teams and organizational settings in remote settings.

Nextcloud is an established tool with a solid track record of use across multiple contexts.
