Open Science Framework is a tool for collaborative work. Free platform for managing research projects with preregistration, data sharing, and collaboration tools. It is particularly relevant in Social Sciences, Education and Healthcare.

Open Science Framework supports open source, distributed and interdisciplinary collaboration and is suited for pair work, small teams and multi-organization networks in remote settings.

Open Science Framework is a well-documented tool with extensive evidence, guides, and community support.
