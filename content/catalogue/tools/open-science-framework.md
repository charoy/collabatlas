Open Science Framework is a tool for collaborative work. Free platform for managing research projects with preregistration, data sharing, and collaboration tools. It is particularly relevant in Social Sciences, Education, Healthcare and Environmental Science.

Open Science Framework supports open source, distributed and interdisciplinary collaboration and is suited for pair work, small teams and multi-organization networks in remote settings.

Open Science Framework is classified as a well-documented tool, indicating broad adoption and available documentation.
