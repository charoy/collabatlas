Obsidian is a tool for collaborative work. Knowledge management tool using linked markdown files for personal and collaborative note-taking. It is particularly relevant in Education, Social Sciences and Publishing.

Obsidian supports co-creation and open source collaboration and is suited for pair work and small teams in remote and hybrid settings.

Obsidian is an established tool with a solid track record of use across multiple contexts.
