Zoom is a cloud-based video conferencing platform that supports real-time video meetings, screen sharing, breakout rooms, and recording capabilities for distributed teams and large-scale virtual events. It became foundational infrastructure for remote collaboration during the COVID-19 pandemic and remains widely adopted across education, business, healthcare, and research sectors.

Zoom's scalable architecture accommodates everything from one-on-one calls to webinars with thousands of participants, and its ease of use has made it a primary tool for synchronous communication in distributed work practices. It is particularly relevant in Business, Education, Healthcare and Social Sciences.

Zoom supports distributed, interdisciplinary and co-production collaboration and is suited for pairs, small teams, organizational settings and multi-organization networks in remote and hybrid settings.

Zoom is classified as a well-documented tool, indicating broad adoption and available documentation. It is a proprietary platform, available on Web, macOS, Windows, iOS, Android and Linux.
