QGIS is a tool for collaborative work. Open-source geographic information system for collaborative spatial data analysis and mapping. It is particularly relevant in Environmental Science, Urban Planning, Citizen Science and Agriculture.

QGIS supports open source, interdisciplinary and community-based collaboration and is suited for small teams and community-scale initiatives in remote and hybrid settings.

QGIS is classified as a well-documented tool, indicating broad adoption and available documentation.
