QGIS is a tool for collaborative work. Open-source geographic information system for collaborative spatial data analysis and mapping. It is particularly relevant in Environmental Science, Urban Planning and Citizen Science.

QGIS supports open source, interdisciplinary and community-based collaboration and is suited for small teams and community-scale initiatives in remote and hybrid settings.

QGIS is a well-documented tool with extensive evidence, guides, and community support.
