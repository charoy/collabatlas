Jamboard is a tool for collaborative work. Google's collaborative digital whiteboard for brainstorming and visual ideation sessions. It is particularly relevant in Education, Design and Business.

Jamboard supports co-design and co-creation collaboration and is suited for pair work and small teams in remote and hybrid settings.

Jamboard is an established tool with a solid track record of use across multiple contexts.
