**Note: Google Jamboard was discontinued in October 2024.** This entry is retained for historical reference. Users are encouraged to consider alternatives such as Miro, FigJam, or other collaborative whiteboard tools.

Jamboard was a collaborative digital whiteboard tool by Google, designed for brainstorming and visual ideation sessions. It was particularly relevant in Education, Design and Business.

Jamboard supported co-design and co-creation collaboration and was suited for pair work and small teams in remote and hybrid settings.
