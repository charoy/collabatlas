Airtable is a tool for collaborative work. Flexible spreadsheet-database hybrid for organizing research data and collaborative project tracking. It is particularly relevant in Business, Social Sciences and Citizen Science.

Airtable supports distributed and co-production collaboration and is suited for small teams and organizational settings in remote settings.

Airtable is an established tool with a solid track record of use across multiple contexts.
