## How It Works

CollabAtlas organizes knowledge about collaboration along multiple dimensions:

- **Domains** — healthcare, education, urban planning, software engineering, and more
- **Collaboration types** — co-design, participatory, distributed, interdisciplinary
- **Scale** — from pairs to communities
- **Research methods** — action research, ethnography, design science
- **Modality** — in-person, remote, hybrid

Every entry is connected to others through these dimensions, enabling discovery across traditional boundaries.
