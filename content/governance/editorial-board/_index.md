The CollabAtlas editorial board is responsible for maintaining the quality, accuracy, and relevance of catalogue entries. Board members serve as domain editors, reviewing contributions in their area of expertise.

## Roles and Responsibilities

### Core Maintainers

Core maintainers own the repository, set strategic direction, and have final authority on governance decisions. They review and merge pull requests that affect platform infrastructure, taxonomies, and schemas.

### Domain Editors

Domain editors are subject-matter experts responsible for reviewing new entries and updates within their domain. Each domain should have at least one active editor. Domain editors:

- Review submitted entries for accuracy and completeness
- Suggest improvements to contributors
- Flag entries that need updating or archiving
- Participate in taxonomy evolution discussions

### Current Domains Seeking Editors

- Healthcare
- Urban Planning

- Agriculture

## Joining the Editorial Board

To become a domain editor, open an issue on the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/collabatlas/collabatlas) with the title "Editorial Board Application — [Your Domain]" and include:

1. Your background and expertise in the domain
2. Examples of collaborative work you have been involved in
3. A brief statement of why you want to contribute to CollabAtlas

Applications are reviewed by core maintainers on a rolling basis.

## Conflict of Interest Policy

Domain editors should disclose any affiliation with tools, methods, or organizations they are reviewing. Editors with a conflict of interest should recuse themselves from reviewing the relevant entry and flag it for another editor.
